Same and different

How the two planets compare

Under the hood, both are large language models: programs that predict text after training on huge amounts of writing. The skills in this guide work on both. Where the buttons, plans or rules differ, every topic has two boxes side by side.

Planet Claude

Made by Anthropic. Apps on the web, desktop and phone. Free plan, then Pro, Max and Ultra, plus Team and Enterprise. You must be 18 or older.

Checked Nov 2026

Planet ChatGPT

Made by OpenAI. Apps on the web, desktop and phone. Free plan, then Go, Plus and Pro, plus Business, Enterprise and Edu. Age 13 and up, with a parent's permission under 18.

Checked Nov 2026
